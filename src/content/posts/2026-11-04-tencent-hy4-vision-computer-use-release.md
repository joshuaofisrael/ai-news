Tencent has released Hy4, the latest version of its Hy large language model, adding the ability to understand images and operate computers for the first time. The company's AI team [announced it on X on 4 November](https://x.com/TencentHunyuan/status/2117870250838630539): "Hy4 is here, new with vision. Firmly among the leading open models."

According to [Tencent's announcement](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-launches-hy4-joining-the-ranks-of-the-worlds-leading-open-source-ai-models/), Hy4 can now handle image tasks such as chart analysis and visual maths, and can work through both command-line tools and graphical interfaces. Tencent says it trained the model on professional software including Blender and CAD to improve long, multi-step tasks.

## Size, access and price

[Tencent's Hy4 research page](https://hy.tencent.ai/research/hy4?langVersion=en) describes a 770-billion-parameter model. It is available through Tencent Cloud's TokenHub, OpenRouter and Tencent apps including CodeBuddy, WorkBuddy, Yuanbao and ima. The listed API price is $0.834 per million input tokens and $2.501 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.042 per million. Tencent is offering 50% off on TokenHub until 31 December, and free use in WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy from 4 to 15 November.

The weights are not out yet. Tencent says it plans to release model weights and system cards "in the coming days", and a smaller Hy4 flash model is also on the way, [TechNode reported](https://technode.com/2026/11/04/tencent-launches-hy4-with-vision-and-computer-use-capabilities/).

## Tencent's own claims, and caveats

Tencent says Hy4 ranks among the leading open-source models and cites internal tests, including a cut in its measured hallucination rate from 12.95% in Hy4 Preview to 7.2%. These are the company's own figures and have not been independently checked. Tencent's research page also acknowledges that a gap remains between Hy4 and the top proprietary models.

On safety, Tencent says the release was built with cybersecurity and biosecurity in mind. [CNA reported](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/tencent-rolls-out-ai-model-trained-limited-cybersecurity-data-amid-hacking-fears-6523736) that the model was trained with limited offensive cybersecurity data amid concerns about AI-assisted hacking.

The launch adds another large Chinese model to a crowded open-model field. Whether Hy4 lives up to Tencent's claims will be easier to judge once the promised weights and system card are public.
