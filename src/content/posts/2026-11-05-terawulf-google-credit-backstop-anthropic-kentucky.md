TeraWulf said on 4 November 2026 that it had signed credit-support agreements with Google LLC tied to debt financing for Anthropic's roughly $19 billion of contracted leases at the Justified Data Campus in Hawesville, Kentucky, and that it had executed a definitive engineering, procurement and construction agreement with Fluor for three buildings at the site. The company [posted the announcement on its investor site](https://investors.terawulf.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/148/terawulf-secures-google-credit-backstop-for-project-financing-in-support-of-19-billion-anthropic-lease-executes-definitive-fluor-epc-agreement) the same morning.

## What the credit support covers

According to TeraWulf's release, Google—described as a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc.—provides contractual credit support for anticipated project debt behind the long-term Justified development. The company cites Alphabet senior unsecured ratings of AA+ (S&P) and Aa2 (Moody's) as context for why that support matters to financing costs. CFO Patrick Fleury is quoted saying cost of capital is fundamental to infrastructure development and that investment-grade credit support strengthens the project's financing profile.

[CoinDesk reported on 5 November](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/05/google-could-take-ownership-stake-in-bitcoin-miner-terawulf-s-ai-project-if-anthropic-defaults) that if Anthropic defaults or becomes insolvent, Google could take over the lease or, in certain cases, pay a termination fee and receive an equity stake in the project vehicle. CoinDesk's account of those step-in terms goes beyond the high-level wording in TeraWulf's press release; readers should treat the press release as the company's own description of the Google agreements and CoinDesk as secondary reporting on the default mechanics. TeraWulf also filed an [8-K dated 4 November 2026](https://investors.terawulf.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-069810/wulf-20261104.htm) covering the transactions.

## Fluor EPC and schedule

TeraWulf said the Fluor EPC covers engineering, procurement, construction, testing and commissioning for three data-center buildings at Justified, expanding a limited notice to proceed announced in March 2026. The release says GMP pricing is being established as engineering and procurement progress, and that phased ready-for-service completions are expected to begin in late 2027 and continue through the second quarter of 2028.

## Why it matters (analysis)

Long-dated AI leases are only as bankable as the credit stack behind them. Pairing Anthropic's contracted demand with Google credit support and a named EPC partner is TeraWulf's attempt to turn a Kentucky campus into financeable infrastructure rather than a speculative build. Delivery still depends on power, labour and whether GMP pricing and milestones hold—risks the company lists in its forward-looking statements. This desk did not independently verify lease economics or Google's contractual step-in rights beyond the cited press release, 8-K and CoinDesk report.
