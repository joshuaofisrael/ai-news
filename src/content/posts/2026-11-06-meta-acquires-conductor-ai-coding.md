Meta has [acquired](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/meta-acquires-ai-coding-startup-conductor/) Conductor, a startup that builds tools for running and coordinating multiple coding agents in parallel. Conductor [said](https://www.conductor.build/announcement) it is joining Meta. Neither side published the price.

Conductor's product is aimed at developers who want several coding agents working on a task together rather than a single chat thread. TechCrunch reported the deal on 4 November 2026 and said financial terms were not disclosed.

## Cloud shutdown and local app support

In its own announcement, Conductor said Conductor Cloud will shut down on 20 November 2026. It said the local app will keep receiving critical updates into early 2027. Users who relied on the hosted service will need another path before that cloud cutoff.

The move fits Meta's broader push into AI assisted software work. The public statements from Conductor and the TechCrunch report are the basis for this piece. They do not describe how Conductor's team will be organised inside Meta, or which Meta products will absorb the technology.

## What is confirmed

Conductor says it is joining Meta. TechCrunch independently reported the acquisition. Cloud shutdown and local app support windows come from Conductor's announcement. Price, headcount, and product roadmap inside Meta were not stated in those sources.

## Caveats

Deal terms remain private. Product plans after the cloud shutdown are not detailed beyond critical updates for the local app. This article does not treat the acquisition as a signal about Meta's competitive position beyond what the named sources say.
