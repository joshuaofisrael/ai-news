Google [posted on X](https://x.com/Google/status/2118390406316728555) on 5 November 2026 that Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, its updated video generation model from Google DeepMind, is rolling out with longer clips, conversational editing and a lower API price. Google AI Studio [posted the same day](https://x.com/GoogleAIStudio/status/2118390015382454491) that the model is available in AI Studio and the Gemini API.

Google’s long-form X post listed the headline changes:

> “Meet Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, our updated video generation model from @GoogleDeepMind … Fast & 30% lower cost: Draft at 360p or 720p ($0.07/s) and upscale to 1080p or 4K … Longer clips: Generate and edit videos up to 15s, or pick any exact length from 3s to 15s … Edit in conversation: Swap backgrounds, subjects, or lighting turn by turn without starting over from scratch … More natural speech: Create fluid, multi-speaker dialogue with clearer lip-sync … More controllable shots: Direct continuous camera moves with fewer jump cuts”

A follow-up [Google post](https://x.com/Google/status/2118390413870412082) said the model is rolling out across Google Cloud, Flow by Google, Google Vids, Google Ads, Google AI Studio and the Gemini API, and to the Gemini app for Google AI subscribers, with 15-second clips available to Ultra.

## What Google’s blogs add

Google’s [The Keyword post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/) (Anish Nangia, Product Manager, Google DeepMind) and a matching [Google Cloud blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-generally-available) say Omni 1.2 Flash is generally available, raises native clip length to 15 seconds from 10 seconds on Omni 1.1, and cuts 720p pricing 30% to $0.07 per second. Both posts say teams can draft at 360p or 720p and upscale selected clips to 1080p or 4K, and that finished outputs carry an imperceptible SynthID watermark. The Cloud post notes Provisioned Throughput for enterprise use and that audio references remain allowlisted.

## Caveats

Feature claims, pricing and availability windows come from Google’s X posts and company blogs dated 5 November 2026. Independent benchmarks of quality, latency or lip-sync were not reviewed for this piece. Treat the 30% price cut and 15-second ceiling as Google’s stated product terms on launch day.
